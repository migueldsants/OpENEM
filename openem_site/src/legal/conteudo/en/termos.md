You can delete your threads and replies at any time, and when you delete your account everything you published goes with it.

- Questions and essay topics come from INEP's official exams and are reproduced as they are.
- The classification of questions by topic and comments generated by artificial intelligence may contain mistakes. The answer key is always the official one.
- Mock exams do not predict your ENEM score.
- Recommendations, goals and schedules are study suggestions, not a guarantee of admission.

- When you open one of these links, you leave OpENEM. The content, availability and use of your data on those sites are the responsibility of each institution, and their own terms and privacy policy apply there.
- Every link is checked by a person before it is added and again periodically; a link that goes a year without a new check leaves the list on its own. Even so, another site may change without notice.
